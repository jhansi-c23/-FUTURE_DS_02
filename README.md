# -FUTURE_DS_02
Customer Retention &amp; Churn Analysis using Power BI to identify high-risk customer segments, tenure-based churn patterns, and key retention drivers with actionable business insights.
📊 Customer Retention & Churn Analysis
🔍 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer subscription data to understand churn behavior and identify retention strategies for a subscription-based business.

Customer churn directly impacts revenue and long-term growth in SaaS and subscription models. The objective of this analysis is to identify high-risk customer segments, analyze retention trends, and provide actionable business recommendations to reduce churn.

This project was completed as part of my Data Science & Analytics Internship – Task 2 (2026).

🎯 Business Problem

Subscription-based businesses often struggle with:

Why are customers leaving?

Which segments are more likely to churn?

How long do customers typically stay?

What factors influence retention?

This project answers these questions using data-driven analysis and interactive dashboards.

📁 Dataset Used

Dataset: Telco Customer Churn Dataset
Source: Kaggle

The dataset includes:

Customer demographics

Contract type

Internet services

Payment methods

Monthly charges

Tenure

Churn status (Yes/No)

🛠 Tools & Technologies Used

Power BI – Dashboard creation & visualization

DAX – Measures & calculated columns

Microsoft Excel – Data cleaning & preprocessing

📊 Dashboard Structure

The interactive Power BI dashboard consists of 4 main pages:

1️⃣ Executive Summary

Total Customers

Overall Churn Rate

Average Tenure

Revenue Loss due to Churn

Churn distribution overview

2️⃣ Customer Segmentation Analysis

Analyzed churn by:

Contract Type

Internet Service

Payment Method

Gender

Senior Citizen status

🔎 Key Insight:
Month-to-month contract customers show significantly higher churn compared to long-term contract users.

3️⃣ Cohort / Tenure Analysis

Customers were grouped into tenure buckets:

0–12 Months

13–24 Months

25–36 Months

37–48 Months

48+ Months

🔎 Key Insight:
Most churn occurs within the first 12 months, indicating early-stage customer drop-off.

4️⃣ Retention Drivers Analysis

Identified key factors influencing retention:

Longer contracts reduce churn

Auto-payment users churn less

Higher monthly charges correlate with higher churn risk

📈 Key Metrics Calculated

Churn Rate (%)

Retention Rate (%)

Average Customer Lifetime (Tenure)

Revenue Loss from Churned Customers

Segment-wise Churn Rate

💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following strategies are recommended:

Offer incentives for long-term contracts

Encourage customers to adopt auto-payment methods

Monitor high-risk customers in first 12 months

Introduce loyalty rewards for retained customers

Provide pricing optimization for high monthly charge users
